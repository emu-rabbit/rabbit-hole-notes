# Emu-rabbit's Rabbit Hole

Emu-rabbit likes programming, games, and writing about her life. These notes bring together a few experiences of learning, creating, dressing, and spending time with others. A project can lead you into a memory; a person can lead you to a moment they shared.

This is a public selection of 58 notes organized with the Rabbit Hole Method. It keeps the pseudonyms and excerpt markings used in the Chinese selection and covers the same material. The English text is a translation, including the quoted passages. The Chinese edition preserves the published Chinese wording.

## Where to begin

- [Profile](00_Entry/Profile.md): meet Emu-rabbit through her life, important relationships, and creative work.
- [Reading map](00_Entry/Reading%20map.md): choose a route by interest, with places to pause along the way.
- [Index](00_Entry/Index.md): find a particular person, story, or project by section.

For events in order, start with the [timeline](20_Life%20chapters/Timeline.md). Narrative footnotes lead to [sources](80_Sources/Source%20index.md), where you can check the words and excerpt boundaries.

The [Rabbit Hole Method](https://github.com/emu-rabbit/rabbit-hole-notes/blob/main/docs/method.en.md) explains the general method. [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) guides AI agents through this selection's scope, question-led lookup, source checks, and action boundaries.

## Download and explore in your own reading app

Choose “Download example” in the English website reader to get this English vault as a ZIP. The Chinese reader offers its own download with the same selection.

1. Extract the ZIP, keeping its folder structure.
2. In Obsidian, choose “Open folder as vault” and select the folder containing README.md, 00_Entry, 10_Self and daily life, 20_Life chapters, and the other sections.
3. Open README.md in reading view and choose the profile or reading map.

No community plugins are needed. The root entry files use relative Markdown links that work on GitHub, the website, and in Obsidian. Use Obsidian or the website reader for double-bracket links and block references in the notes. A plain text editor can also open the Markdown.
