# Emu-rabbit's Rabbit Hole

Emu-rabbit likes programming, games, and writing about her life. These notes bring together a few experiences of learning, creating, dressing, and spending time with others. A project can lead you into a memory; a person can lead you to a moment they shared.

This is a public selection of 58 notes organized with the Rabbit Hole Method. It keeps the pseudonyms and excerpt markings used in the Chinese selection and covers the same material. The English text is a translation, including the quoted passages. The Chinese edition preserves the published Chinese wording.

## Follow a connection

The main notes tell stories in the third person. Footnotes lead to [[80_Sources/Source index|sources]], which distinguish records from the time, later interviews, and the scope of each excerpt. You can stay with a story or take another step and compare an earlier record with a later recollection.

This selection uses one index for all its sections. It has no separate story or creative-work index, relationship circles, or maintenance section. The [Rabbit Hole Method](https://github.com/emu-rabbit/rabbit-hole-notes/blob/main/docs/method.en.md) describes a fuller structure that can grow with your own material. Agents can start with the [[AGENTS|reading guide]].

## Download and explore in your own reading app

Choose “Download example” in the English website reader to get this English vault as a ZIP. The Chinese reader offers its own download with the same selection.

1. Extract the ZIP, keeping its folder structure.
2. In Obsidian, choose “Open folder as vault” and select the folder containing README.md, 00_Entry, 10_Self and daily life, 20_Life chapters, and the other sections.
3. Open README.md in reading view and begin with one of the short routes above.

No community plugins are needed. A plain text editor can also read the Markdown. Double-bracket links and block references work in Obsidian and the website reader; GitHub's file view does not turn all of them into clickable links.
