## 2022–2024: learning and projects

### What she learns stays

Source: a Facebook post the author wrote in 2022. Two excerpts from her own words.

> I feel that technical skills can sometimes be quite reassuring.
> What you get reflects how much time and effort you put in.
> Once you have learned it, you have learned it. You do not have to worry about it running away one day (?)

> When I want to make toys for myself to play with, not having a foundation in design or backend skills really limits many of the toys I want to make.

^learning-skills

### Beyond frontend

Source: a Facebook post the author wrote in 2024. Excerpt from her own words.

> This year, I even made a major change in my professional field.
> As a frontend engineer, I stepped beyond that field to learn Flutter and Firebase.
> I worked through the growing pains at the beginning, step by step, and learned the technologies properly.
> Firebase gave me some backend capabilities, so there were more projects I could make.
> I also took a basic color-theory class and learned the very basics of design.
> I am even considering building more skills in design and drawing.

^learning-later
