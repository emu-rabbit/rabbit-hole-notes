English translation of the published Chinese excerpts. Quotations below are translations, not verbatim English originals.

## High school: comparison and computing

Source: interview with the author, 2026-10-29. Excerpts from her own words in two answers.

> My brilliant high-school peers were my biggest source of anxiety and feelings of inferiority, but they were very good people. We are still close, and I still look back fondly on my high-school life.

> But in fact, I really do enjoy computing and software engineering. As I said, I like solving problems, and being able to use a computer, such a fast and precise tool, to solve problems is something that fascinates me.

## One-click reports at work

Source: interview with the author, 2026-10-29. Adapted excerpt; square brackets generalize the system's name.

> We had to copy from [the work system] and paste into a group to report things. The time, reason, host, and other details were scattered around, so copying them manually was a hassle. This plugin provided one-click copying in an easy-to-read format that could quickly be pasted into the reporting group. Our whole operations team was using it at the time.

^work-oneclick

## FoodPower: gameplay and development

Source: interview with the author, 2026-10-30. Excerpt from her own words.

> Anyway, as someone who likes playing, I tried making my own mod, bringing power through food. I felt Minecraft food should have freer gameplay, with combinations as flexible as TinkerConstruct.

> But honestly, I only knew a little Java and had no mod-development experience. There was no AI assistance back then, and tutorials were scarce and hard to get through. When Minecraft entered a period of frequent version updates, the environment for developing mods became even harder, and there were even fewer tutorials.

^foodpower-origin

## FoodPower: the image-compositing hurdle

Source: interview with the author, 2026-10-30. Excerpt from her own words.

I struggled for a long time and finally solved it. I think I mentioned it on Facebook. It was a function for combining and flattening several PNG images into the appearance of a Minecraft item.
