> I had wanted to make a portfolio for a long time, but could not find the idea. Finally, during a shower, the word “macaron” popped into my head. There is a macaron shop I consider the best: distinctive designs, generous fillings, each macaron with its own character, and most importantly, not too sweet. Just like the macarons you see above, I brought that into every corner of this portfolio. But that shop has already closed. By putting it in my portfolio, I also hoped to have somewhere to remember the six years it gave me.
