# 2025–2026: Creating and being seen

In recent years, Emu-rabbit made an [[50_Creative work/Independent work/Frozen Rabbit Workshop|FF14 materials-planning tool]], a [[50_Creative work/Independent work/Frozen Rabbit Tome|gathering tool]], and [[50_Creative work/Independent work/Boundary Notes|Boundary Notes]], as well as a personal website and portfolio. AI made it easier to start tools she had previously considered too much trouble and helped new projects take shape faster. At the same time, she began caring more about keeping her own character in her work.[^1]

## Player feedback and design choices
The Workshop turns nested recipes into a flat materials list. Many players thanked her for it, strengthening her belief that software should serve people. Frozen Rabbit Tome has few users, but she still likes it because making it taught her to reduce cognitive load, handle visual noise, and balance different resources.[^2]

## Boundary Notes
She wants users to organize their preferences and boundaries at their own pace, keep the file to revisit, and send it to someone only when they want to share it.[^3]

Promotion met with a few small setbacks. Looking back, though, the secret-keeping rabbit's RPG-style conversational company and the detail of its roughly three hundred items remain elements she wants to preserve.[^4]

## Writing about herself in her own words
When making her personal website, she [[30_Stories/Chapters/Keeping her own words when working with AI|wrote her self-introduction herself]], asked AI not to change it, and arranged the layout to her own rhythm. Her portfolio became [[30_Stories/Chapters/Six years of macarons in a portfolio|a box of macarons]], remembering the six years a now-closed shop had given her.[^6]

She also wrote the [Rabbit Hole Method](https://github.com/emu-rabbit/rabbit-hole-notes/blob/main/docs/method.en.md), wanting future readers or agents to get to know her through experiences, relationships, stories, and creative work, with continued updates as new memories and interviews emerge.[^5]
