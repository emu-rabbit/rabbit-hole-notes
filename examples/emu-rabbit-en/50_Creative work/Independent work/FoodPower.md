# FoodPower

FoodPower is a food-powered mod Emu-Rabbit made for Minecraft. She wanted food to be freely combinable, like TinkerConstruct, and let players gain power from cooking, so she tried making her own mod.[^1]

## Starting with just a little Java
At the time, she knew only a little Java and had no mod-development experience. There was no AI help, tutorials were scarce and hard to understand, and Minecraft versions changed frequently, making the development environment harder to get to grips with.[^1]

She particularly remembers the image-compositing hurdle: she wanted to combine multiple PNGs into the appearance of a Minecraft item, but could not understand the source code or find tutorials to refer to. It took her a long time to work it out, and she remembers writing about it on Facebook.[^2]

A separate development post from 2020 records how she spent a day and a half trying different parameters until [[30_Stories/Chapters/The function finally returns true|a function finally returned true]].[^3]
