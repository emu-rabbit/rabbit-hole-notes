# Scientific Romance

“Scientific Romance” is the poetry-recitation text Emu-Rabbit wrote for her class in high school. She also led her classmates in taking part in the recitation.[^1]

She began enjoying writing modern poetry in junior high and continued in high school. Her Chinese-language teacher liked her and would discuss her writing with her. Later, she was the one who wrote the recitation text on behalf of the class.[^1]
