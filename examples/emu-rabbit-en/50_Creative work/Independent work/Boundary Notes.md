# Boundary Notes

Boundary Notes is Emu-Rabbit's website for organizing BDSM preferences and boundaries. She found few existing websites to choose from and felt their user experience was poor, so she organized hundreds of items into the tool she wanted to make. She cares about the user experience and privacy, and also considered how a backend requiring no login could avoid abuse.[^1]

## A private notebook and the secret-keeping rabbit
The secret-keeping rabbit accompanies users through conversation as they sort through their feelings at their own pace. Users can keep their note files close and return when their feelings, boundaries, or lives change. When they want to share, they can create a private link to give to someone.[^2]

## After setbacks in promotion
Promotion met with a few small setbacks. Looking back, though, the secret-keeping rabbit's RPG-style conversational company and the detail of roughly three hundred included items remain the elements and presentation she wants for the project.[^3]

Before making this project, she had already tried preserving her own words and layout rhythm on her [[30_Stories/Chapters/Keeping her own words when working with AI|personal website]]. She brought that experience into Boundary Notes.[^5]

## Question bank and asset licensing
The source code and published question bank use the MIT license. Branding, images such as the secret-keeping rabbit, and other specified assets have separate permission requirements. A reference to an image filename in the question bank does not mean that image uses the same license.[^4]
