# Making things

Emu-Rabbit wanted to become an engineer because she liked solving problems. She is fascinated by the moment a difficulty of her own or someone else's is resolved, and believes this is where the value of software lies. Games, technical curiosity, and memories she wants to keep can also lead her to start creating.[^1][^3][^5]

## Making tools that help
In operations work, she turned reports that required repeatedly assembling fields into a [[30_Stories/Chapters/Turning repeated reports into one click|one-click copy extension]] the whole team could use. Playing FF14, she made the [[50_Creative work/Independent work/Frozen Rabbit Workshop|Workshop]] to address the hassle of organizing materials, flattening nested recipes into a list. Receiving players' thanks and affection was when she felt the greatest sense of achievement.[^2]

## Building ideas she likes
[[50_Creative work/Independent work/FoodPower|FoodPower]] began with her wish for freely combinable cooking in Minecraft. [[50_Creative work/Independent work/LinkArray|LinkArray]] came from her fondness for linked lists: she wondered whether they could be combined with arrays and set about implementing it.[^3]

About six years later, she asked AI to review LinkArray. Even after learning there were better options for its original goal, she still loved it. [[50_Creative work/Independent work/Frozen Rabbit Tome|Frozen Rabbit Tome]] also has few users, but the design trade-offs she learned while making it keep it dear to her.[^4]

## Words and memories
In high school, she wrote [[50_Creative work/Independent work/Scientific Romance|“Scientific Romance”]] and led her classmates in a recitation. Later, when making her portfolio, she put [[30_Stories/Chapters/Six years of macarons in a portfolio|a macaron shop she had loved for six years]] into the website. Her golden hamster [[40_Important relationships/Pets/Mengmeng|Mengmeng]] continued to live on in nAnB after passing away.[^5]

## Keeping her own way of doing things
Promoting [[50_Creative work/Independent work/Boundary Notes|Boundary Notes]] met with a few small setbacks. Looking back, the secret-keeping rabbit's company and the detail of the content still represent what she wants to express.[^6]

AI lets her make new things faster, but she also cares more about keeping her own character in her work. When making her personal website, she [[30_Stories/Chapters/Keeping her own words when working with AI|wrote the text herself and asked AI not to change it]], then arranged the paragraphs and the rhythm of the layout herself.[^7]
