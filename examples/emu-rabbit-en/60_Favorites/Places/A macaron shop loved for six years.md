# A macaron shop loved for six years

There was a shop Emu-Rabbit considered the best macaron shop. She loved its distinctive designs, generous fillings, the individual character of each macaron, and most importantly, that they were not too sweet.[^1]

The shop later closed. Emu-Rabbit brought it into her [[30_Stories/Chapters/Six years of macarons in a portfolio|macaron-shaped portfolio]], wanting a place to remember the six years the shop had given her.[^1]
