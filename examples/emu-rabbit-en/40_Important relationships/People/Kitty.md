# Kitty

Kitty is Emu-Rabbit's current partner and her human kitty. In her self-introduction, Emu-Rabbit describes her partner as clingy and sees Kitty as someone deeply important with whom she wants to spend her life.[^1]

Kitty takes housework seriously and is often busy doing things even during dates. Sometimes Emu-Rabbit wants more interaction, so she explicitly says [[30_Stories/Chapters/A date without chores this time|no housework on this date]], keeping the time for the two of them to spend together.[^2]
