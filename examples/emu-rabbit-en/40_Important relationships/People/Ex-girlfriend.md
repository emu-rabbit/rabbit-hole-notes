# Ex-girlfriend

Emu-Rabbit's ex-girlfriend was once her master. Looking back on the relationship, she still remembers the happiness of watching the stars together, as well as questions she has never been able to resolve since they parted.[^1][^2]

## Watching the stars together
Emu-Rabbit once told a friend that happiness was probably wishing this very moment would keep going. [[30_Stories/Chapters/Lying under the stars again|Lying outdoors watching the stars]] with her ex-girlfriend, she felt that way again.[^1]

## After they parted
Later, she often asked herself what she could do if they went through it again, or whether they would ultimately reach the same ending. When recalling the relationship, she still had not worked it out.[^2]
