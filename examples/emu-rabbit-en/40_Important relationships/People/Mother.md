# Mother

Emu-Rabbit feels that her mother has her own realizations, feelings, and changes, and does not think of her as a traditional woman. As for the answers within those changes, she feels only her mother knows.[^2]

She once wrote about [[30_Stories/Chapters/Wanting to go home for dinner again|wanting to go home for dinner more often]], finding that she could finally smile and tell her mother she would come back for meals more often.[^1]
