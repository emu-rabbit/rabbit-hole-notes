# 2011–2014: Mathematics, words, and computing

In high school, Emu-Rabbit had things she wanted to do in mathematics, computing, and writing. She enjoyed investigating mathematical problems and was fascinated by using computers to solve problems. She had started writing modern poetry in junior high and continued in high school.[^1][^2][^3]

## Classmates and comparing abilities
Her exceptionally talented peers made her feel panicked and inferior. But she also remembers them as wonderful people with whom she shared close bonds, and she still loved high school life.[^1]

## The four-color problem and computing
She did a project on the four-color problem, a subject she particularly liked. After she submitted the research, little came of it, and she felt it had not received serious attention from others. Later in high school, her focus gradually shifted from mathematical research toward computer science.[^2]

Working in computing and software engineering really did make her happy. She liked computers as fast, precise tools, and was especially fascinated by using them to solve problems.[^1]

## Writing poetry and class recitation
Her Chinese-language teacher liked her and would discuss her writing with her. She later wrote [[50_Creative work/Independent work/Scientific Romance|“Scientific Romance”]] as the text for her class's poetry recitation and led her classmates in taking part.[^3]
