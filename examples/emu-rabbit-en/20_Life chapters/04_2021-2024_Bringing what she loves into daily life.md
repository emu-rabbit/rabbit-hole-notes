# 2021–2024: Bringing what she loves into daily life

During this period, Emu-rabbit attended a work party wearing clothes she liked. She also kept learning, moving beyond frontend development into Flutter, Firebase, and basic color design.[^1][^2]

## An answer at a work party
Someone asked whether she was cosplaying. She said she was not; she simply liked wearing women's clothes. It was a conversation she still remembered when recalling [[30_Stories/Chapters/Wearing what she likes to a work party|that work party]].[^1]

## Moving beyond the frontend
In 2022, she wrote that technical skills made her feel secure: what she spent time and effort learning would stay with her. She also found that when she wanted to make things to play with, lacking design fundamentals and backend skills limited much of what she wanted to do.[^2]

In 2024, she wrote that she had branched out into Flutter and Firebase. She had gradually made it through the initial growing pains, and Firebase gave her some backend capabilities, expanding the projects she could build. She also took a basic color theory course and considered continuing to develop her design and drawing skills.[^2]
