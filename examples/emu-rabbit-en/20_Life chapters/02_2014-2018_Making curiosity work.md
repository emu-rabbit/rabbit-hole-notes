# 2014–2018: Making curiosity work

At university, Emu-rabbit was very interested in simulation. Watching programs being translated and interpreted, with everything following rules and behaving precisely, fascinated her.[^1]

## A computer inside a computer
She once wanted to write a “computer inside a computer,” something like a sandbox or virtual machine. Looking back, she described herself then as just a “beginner” who could not write much properly, and she did not continue the project.[^1]

## The infinite hotel assignment
She built an infinite hotel for an assignment using linked lists and big numbers. It could book enormously large room numbers: the worry was not running out of rooms, but running out of space on the computer. Later, her teacher found a bug. She felt a little disappointed and remembers that she probably never fixed it.[^2]

The arrays and linked lists she learned about in university later gave her the idea of combining them. In 2020, she made [[50_Creative work/Independent work/LinkArray|LinkArray]].[^3]
