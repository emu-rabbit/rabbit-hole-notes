# 2019–2020: Finding reasons to make things

After starting work in operations, Emu-Rabbit often had to paste scattered information such as times, reasons, and hosts into a group chat to report it. She made a [[30_Stories/Chapters/Turning repeated reports into one click|one-click copy extension]] that turned the manual assembly into readable report text. Eventually, the entire operations team used it.[^1]

## A stable life and small achievements
In 2019, she wrote that a stable life was slowly rebuilding her heart. Doing simple things, finishing them, and seeing results also helped her gradually regain the confidence she had lost.[^2]

## Finding her way with FoodPower
She also tried making [[50_Creative work/Independent work/FoodPower|FoodPower]], wanting Minecraft cooking to allow free combinations and give players power. At the time, she knew only a little Java and had no experience developing mods. Tutorials were scarce, versions changed frequently, and she had to figure out much of it herself.[^3]

In a 2020 post, she recorded [[30_Stories/Chapters/The function finally returns true|a function that took a day and a half to return true]]. Years later, when discussing development, she still remembered the hurdle that took her so long to clear when combining multiple PNGs into the appearance of an item.[^3][^5]

## Combining arrays and linked lists
Also in 2020, she recalled the data structures she had learned at university and tried making [[50_Creative work/Independent work/LinkArray|LinkArray]]. She was very satisfied with what her effort produced, but did not know where to publish it or how to find related research.[^4]
