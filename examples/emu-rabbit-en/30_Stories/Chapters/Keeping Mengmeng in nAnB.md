# Keeping Mengmeng in nAnB

Emu-Rabbit made a chatroom-style number-guessing game, [[50_Creative work/Independent work/nAnB|nAnB]], and put her [[40_Important relationships/Pets/Mengmeng|golden hamster Mengmeng]] into it. She introduced the hamster in the project README: “Mengmeng is my golden hamster. Her name is Mengmeng, but that goes without saying.”[^1]

The picture in the game was taken by chance. Mengmeng was sitting in a corner of her cage, with her back to the camera, looking at the wall. Emu-Rabbit thought she looked a little like a world-weary middle-aged man. She found it funny and really liked the picture.[^3]

Mengmeng later passed away. Emu-Rabbit wrote in her portfolio: “Mengmeng has already gone to be a little angel, but lives on forever in my work like this.”[^2]

To get to know Mengmeng beyond the picture, continue to [[40_Important relationships/Pets/Mengmeng#Exploring the room|her everyday explorations of the room]].
