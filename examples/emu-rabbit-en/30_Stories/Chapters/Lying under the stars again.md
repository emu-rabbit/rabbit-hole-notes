# Lying under the stars again

A friend asked Emu-Rabbit what she thought happiness was. She said it was probably wanting this very moment to keep going just as it was.[^1]

Lying outdoors watching the stars with [[40_Important relationships/People/Ex-girlfriend|her ex-girlfriend]], she felt happy once again. When she later spoke about that time together, watching the stars was something she remembered especially clearly.[^1]
