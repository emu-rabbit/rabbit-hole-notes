# Caring takes energy and rest

Caring for the people around her takes a lot of Emu-Rabbit's energy. She describes continuing to shoulder it, then retreating to rest and write down her feelings when she runs out of energy. Once her strength returns, she comes back to keep caring for the people around her.[^1]
