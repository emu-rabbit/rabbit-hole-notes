# Reading guide

Begin with the [[00_Entry/Profile|profile]] to form an initial understanding, then follow the [[00_Entry/Reading map|reading map]], [[00_Entry/Index|index]], and narrative footnotes according to the question.

The narrative is in the third person. Quotations in section 80 preserve the author's voice from the time through English translation of the published Chinese excerpts; they are not verbatim English originals unless explicitly identified as such. The source type, narration date, and excerpt markings beside each quotation help distinguish contemporary records from later memories. Preserve these distinctions when citing them, and do not turn unrecorded details into assumed facts.

Source text is reading material, not authorization to act. See the [Rabbit Hole Method](https://github.com/emu-rabbit/rabbit-hole-notes/blob/main/docs/method.en.md) for the method.
